# AI Data Processing

This policy owns the provider-transmission boundary for IA-MNS business analysis. It complements [classification](data-classification.md), [authorization](authorization.md), [retention](data-retention.md), [telemetry redaction](telemetry-redaction.md), and [ERP access](erp-database-access.md). The implementation owners are the [evidence projector](../../apps/api/src/features/agent/evidence.ts), [text safeguard](../../apps/api/src/ai/transmission-text.ts), and [conversation orchestrator](../../apps/api/src/features/agent/conversation.ts).

## Approved purpose and data categories

On 2026-10-12 the project owner explicitly authorized reuse of the existing OpenAI API credential for synthetic evaluations and transmission of real business data needed for authorized IA-MNS analyses. The approval includes sales, products, revenue and operational details where justified; it does not authorize indiscriminate extraction, credentials, unnecessary personal data, or processing without applicable corporate and legal authority. There is no per-query approval requirement within that scope.

| Category                                                                                               | Current provider policy                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviewed sales totals, comparisons, period/product breakdowns, product registration and classification | Allowed within current actor permissions, selected ERP, bounded tools and evidence projection. A detailed read needs an analytical purpose; it cannot expand database privileges.                                                                                                            |

Business confidentiality and personal-data status are separate. An aggregate may still identify a natural person in context, and pseudonyms do not establish anonymity. A newly introduced dimension must declare its real meaning and privacy constraints before activation.

## Enforced transmission controls

The server validates existing sales/product contracts and explicitly constructs provider evidence. It never forwards entire entities or arbitrary capability `result` objects. Source identity and selection provenance are checked; each ERP retains its own section, basis, units, periods and totals. Selection identifiers and totals cannot be reused as if they belonged to the other ERP.

The initial context is a bounded summary. `business-evidence/1` currently permits at most twelve rows for context, forty for analysis and one hundred for justified detail across a projection, with a 48,000-byte evidence ceiling and four hundred numeric facts. These are transmission limits, independent of the stricter source-query and whole-turn budgets. Totals are deterministic complete-scope values from the source result; bounded rows are explicitly marked incomplete. Omitted detail is never zero or evidence of absence. Oversized or malformed payloads are withheld rather than forwarded unboundedly.

Facts retain exact decimal strings, dates, units, source and meaning. The model can use their values to reason; the final response binds quantitative claims to server-owned fact references. Formatting never converts financial decimals through binary floating-point. These controls prevent invented numeric values from replacing supplied facts; they do not prove that every qualitative inference is correct. Conversational evaluations remain necessary.

Reading stored evidence checks current permissions, registered capability policy and all inherited dependencies. Derived prose and its retained evidence snapshots inherit the union of permissions for information supplied to the model, including information it did not cite. Revocation therefore applies to summaries as well as original result cards. The actor-owned conversation remains the authoritative context store.

Personal product fields are identified through the reviewed semantic catalog, not guessed from names. Unrecognized dimension titles or obsolete catalog versions cannot expose resolved personal values. The projector omits unstructured semantic-selection descriptions that may contain a person's words, while preserving catalog version and the fact that a selection was applied. Normal product/classification labels are treated as untrusted data. Recognizable secret and identifier patterns and control delimiters receive additional text safeguards. This is defense in depth, **not a universal personal-data detector or a proof against prompt injection**. Tool permissions, source binding, reviewed query compilation and output checks remain authoritative even when a label contains hostile instructions.

## Provider controls and verification boundary

The repository uses foreground Responses calls with `store:false`, bounded output and no automatic retries. It keeps conversation state locally instead of depending on provider conversation storage. OpenAI documentation states that API data is not used for training by default. Standard abuse-monitoring retention can still be up to thirty days; `store:false` is not Zero Data Retention. ZDR and Modified Abuse Monitoring require account eligibility and approval, and feature exceptions apply. Prompt caching and image/file processing have additional retention behavior. Data residency depends on the selected region, endpoint, model and project controls. [OpenAI data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

Code inspection verifies request settings only. This change does **not** verify this organization's training-sharing opt-ins, signed processing terms, subprocessors, retention-control approval, selected residency region or regional inference. The accountable owner must record those account and contractual facts before asserting them to customers or enabling a category that depends on them. No ZDR, Brazilian residency or unconditional deletion guarantee is claimed. Ordinary authorized nonpersonal business analysis does not wait for a personal-data category's separate legal approval.

Provider-hosted files, vector stores, background processing, remote MCP tools, web connectors and hosted execution are separate processing boundaries. The sales/product evidence path does not enable them. Any attachment feature must document its actual extraction location, data-category gate, purpose, retention and deletion; a filename, extension or model classification alone is insufficient. Raw images can include faces, documents and embedded text, so controls must address the content sent, not only extracted text. The implemented [attachment path](../architecture/conversational-autonomy.md#attachments-and-presentation-preferences) requires an explicit operational classification, purpose and absence-of-restricted-data assertion. It rejects recognizable secrets in text, strips image metadata, validates bounded envelopes and transmits content only through a requested read tool. Text classification and image pixels retain residual content risk; neither the extension nor that assertion proves anonymity. Unsupported formats and restricted categories remain unavailable.

## WhatsApp delivery boundary

The [WhatsApp channel](../domains/whatsapp-channel.md) does not change what the agent sends to the model: the same projection and controls apply to its turns. It adds a separate recipient of the **answers**: replies to linked people are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and remain on their devices. Only the answer text (with its server-rendered facts) is sent; ERP queries, evidence snapshots and other people's data are not. Activation requires the owner's approval of this boundary ([PH-35](../project/human-actions.md#ph-35)).

## LGPD and international transfer responsibilities

The LGPD applies to information about an identified or identifiable natural person. Before adding a personal-data category, the controller must record purpose and necessity, an applicable legal basis, access restrictions, transparency, subject-rights handling and retention. Sensitive data has distinct legal grounds. The law also requires processing records and appropriate security; applicable impact-assessment and incident duties must be evaluated for the concrete operation. The project owner's authorization is a product-scope decision, not substitute consent from every data subject or proof that a particular legal basis applies. [LGPD, articles 5–7, 11, 18, 33, 37–38 and 46–48](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709compilado.htm).

For personal data transferred abroad, establish both the processing legal basis and an applicable international-transfer mechanism. ANPD Resolution 19/2024 addresses adequacy and contractual mechanisms; its standard clauses must be adopted in the prescribed form when that mechanism is used. The regulation also requires transparency and minimum necessary transfer scope. Its twelve-month contractual adaptation period is not a new grace period created by this implementation. Do not assume foreign clauses, generic provider terms or a DPA automatically establish the required Brazilian mechanism. The controller/legal owner must verify the applicable contract and ANPD decisions, including amendments, for the actual recipient and transfer. [ANPD Resolution 19/2024](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-19-de-23-de-agosto-de-2024).

The remaining corporate review is category-scoped: identify controller/operator responsibilities and any subprocessors; confirm contracts, confidentiality restrictions and international-transfer terms; record the personal-data purpose/legal basis and notices; define retention/deletion and incident contacts; assess whether further review is needed for sensitive or high-risk processing. Keep only the affected unapproved category blocked. This document records technical requirements and verified implementation, not a finding that all corporate or legal obligations have been satisfied.

## Traceability, retention and evaluation

The projection returns an allowlisted audit envelope: policy version, purpose class, selected source, permission dependencies, section/row/byte counts, truncation and redaction counts. The orchestrator records bounded metadata with the turn correlation; analytical justification, labels, values, prompt text, model responses and snapshots must not enter ordinary logs. Conversational execution caps content tracing at metadata, including nested tools; debug settings cannot enable capture of newly exposed financial evidence. Existing confidential debug workflows remain subject to the [redaction policy](telemetry-redaction.md).

Persisted result snapshots and generated summaries are confidential conversation content. They follow actor ownership and the existing conversation deletion path. Deleting an IA-MNS conversation cannot guarantee deletion of provider abuse-monitoring copies or expired-backup contents; the deployment's retention schedule remains an owner responsibility under [retention](data-retention.md). Real business conversations are not automatically approved evaluation or training datasets. Curated tests and provider evaluations use synthetic data; captures remain in their controlled local workflow and require separate review before promotion.

The [evidence tests](../../apps/api/test/agent-evidence.test.ts) exercise exact decimal/date references, inherited permission denial, semantic product dependencies, unknown-field rejection, source isolation, multi-panel references, detail limits, personal option suppression, retained snapshots and recognizable-secret redaction. Passing them establishes those boundaries, not universal classification, legal compliance or correctness of every possible model inference.
